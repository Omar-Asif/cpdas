Courier and Parcel Delivery Analytics System

A read-only analytics and reporting system for Dhrutogoti Courier's operations manager. CPDAS analyses parcel, scan, rider, zone and deposit records that are already captured elsewhere — it never adds, updates or deletes them. Choose a module from the sidebar to get started.

M1 · Delivery Performance

Success rate, on-time %, average delivery time per zone.
M2 · Rider Productivity

Parcels per duty day and failed-attempt rate, per rider.
M3 · COD Reconciliation

Cash collected vs. deposited per rider, per day.
M4 · AI Zone Matching

Cosine-similarity address-to-zone matching and dispatcher overrides.
M5 · Reporting

Printable A4 reports R1–R5.
